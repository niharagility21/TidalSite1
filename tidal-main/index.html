FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to get started?
Most customers are live within one week. We connect to your existing email inbox, map your product catalogue, and configure your margin rules. No complex IT project.
What if an RFQ has items we don't stock?
Tidal flags unmatched items and routes them to your team for manual pricing. The rest of the quote — often 90%+ of line items — is handled automatically.
Do you integrate with our existing systems?
Yes. Tidal integrates with common ERP and inventory systems via API or CSV export. We also work as a standalone tool with no integration required.
How does pricing work?
Tidal starts at $2,500/month for up to 500 RFQs/month. Enterprise plans with custom volume, dedicated support, and SLA guarantees are available. No setup fee.
Is our quote data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share your pricing, catalogue, or customer data with third parties. SOC 2 compliance in progress.
